## 1. Product Definition

A hair system is a custom-engineered cranial prosthesis made from 100% human hair,
hand-tied or injected into an ultra-thin base. It is designed to replace hair lost
to male pattern baldness (androgenetic alopecia) with a completely natural,
undetectable result.

**What it is NOT:** a wig, a toupee, a hairpiece, a helmet, or synthetic.
Every system is custom-made to the client's exact head shape, hair color,
density, curl pattern, and attachment preference.

## 2. Base Types

### Ultra-Thin Skin (0.03mm)
- Thickness: 0.03mm polyurethane
- Appearance: Near-invisible edge, scalp-like translucency
- Realism: Maximum (10/10)
- Durability: Low (3/10)
- Lifespan: 2–3 months
- Best for: Exposed hairlines, front hairline realism, slicked-back styles
- Care: Gentle cleaning only. Avoid aggressive adhesives. Replace tape every 1–3 days.
- Cost: Premium

### Thin Skin (0.06–0.08mm)
- Thickness: 0.06–0.08mm polyurethane
- Appearance: Barely visible edge, good scalp realism
- Realism: High (8/10)
- Durability: Medium (6/10)
- Lifespan: 3–4 months
- Best for: Good realism with more durability than ultra-thin
- Care: Standard cleaning. Tape change every 3–5 days.
- Cost: Standard

### Swiss Lace
- Material: Fine Swiss lace mesh
- Appearance: Most undetectable lace. Mesh disappears against scalp.
- Realism: Maximum (10/10)
- Durability: Low (3/10)
- Lifespan: 1.5–2 months
- Best for: Maximum undetectability, all-over ventilation, hot climates, exposed hairlines
- Care: Delicate handling. Lace-specific cleaning products. Never pull or stretch.
- Cost: Premium

### French Lace
- Material: Durable French lace mesh
- Appearance: Slightly more visible texture than Swiss. Still very natural.
- Realism: Good (7/10)
- Durability: Medium (6/10)
- Lifespan: 2–3 months
- Best for: Balance of realism and durability
- Care: Standard lace cleaning. More forgiving than Swiss.
- Cost: Standard

### Monofilament (Mono)
- Material: Monofilament polyester mesh
- Appearance: Visible perimeter texture. Less undetectable.
- Realism: Moderate (5/10)
- Durability: Maximum (9/10)
- Lifespan: 4–6 months
- Best for: Active clients, swimming, sports, maximum durability
- Care: Very durable. Standard cleaning. Holds up to sweat and chlorine.
- Cost: Economy

### Hybrid (Skin Front + Lace Body)
- Construction: Ultra-thin skin at front 2cm + lace mesh body
- Appearance: Undetectable hairline + breathable crown
- Realism: Excellent (9/10)
- Durability: Medium (5/10)
- Lifespan: 2–3 months
- Best for: Exposed hairlines with all-day comfort
- Care: Two-material protocol. Gentle on front skin, standard on lace body.
- Cost: Premium

## 3. Hair Types

### Indian Remy
- Strand diameter: 0.06–0.08mm (medium)
- Natural color: Dark brown to black (#1B–#3)
- Wave: Slight natural body wave
- Best for: Most Caucasian and Hispanic clients. Excellent value. Most popular.
- Durability: Good. Cuticle-aligned (Remy) prevents tangling.
- Cost: Standard

### European
- Strand diameter: 0.04–0.06mm (fine)
- Natural color: Light brown to dark blonde (#4–#7)
- Wave: Naturally straight to slight wave
- Best for: Clients with naturally fine, straight hair. Lightest density feel.
- Durability: Good. Finer strand needs gentler brushing.
- Cost: Premium (highest)

### Chinese Remy
- Strand diameter: 0.08–0.10mm (thick/coarse)
- Natural color: Jet black to dark brown (#1–#2)
- Wave: Naturally very straight. Resists curling.
- Best for: Clients with naturally coarse, straight hair. Asian clients. Most durable.
- Durability: Excellent. Thickest strand = longest lifespan.
- Cost: Standard

### Brazilian Remy
- Strand diameter: 0.07–0.09mm (medium-coarse)
- Natural color: Dark brown (#2–#4)
- Wave: Natural body wave. Holds curl well.
- Best for: Clients wanting volume and body. Caribbean, Mediterranean, Hispanic clients.
- Durability: Very good. Strong strand, good wave retention.
- Cost: Premium (moderate)

## 6. Density Scale

Density is measured as a percentage of what would be considered "full biological
density" for a man of that age.

| Range | Label | Typical Age |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 70–80% | Light | 50+ |
| 80–90% | Medium-Light | 40–50 |
| 90–100% | Medium | 25–40 |
| 100–110% | Medium-Heavy | 20–30 |
| 110–120% | Heavy | Under 25 |

**Warning:** Density above 120% looks unnatural and helmet-like. Never recommend.

## 7. Front Hairline Graduation

The front hairline is the single most important factor in undetectability.
A detectable hairline ruins the entire system.

### The leading 0.5cm MUST have:
1. **Graduated density**: 60–65% at 0.5cm, decreasing to ~40% at the very edge
2. **Single bleached knots**: Every knot is individually bleached. No dark dots visible.
3. **Baby hairs**: Fine, wispy, irregularly placed hairs at 0.2–0.5cm. Not uniform.
4. **Scalp pore reflection**: The skin base is transparent enough that natural scalp
   texture shows through. Pores, slight variations in skin tone — it must look like
   hair growing FROM scalp, not sitting ON it.

### What ruins a hairline:
- **Blunt edge**: A straight, sharp cutoff where the base ends. Dead giveaway.
- **Visible lace grid**: Any mesh pattern visible through the hairline.
- **Dark knots**: Unbleached knots create a visible dotted line.
- **Uniform density**: Perfectly even density looks artificial. Nature is irregular.
- **No baby hairs**: A hairline without fine, wispy edge hairs looks painted on.

## 8. Attachment Methods

### Daily Wear (Tape)
- Products: Walker No-Shine Tape, Walker Tape Liner
- Hold: 1 day
- Routine: Apply tape to perimeter. Remove nightly. Clean base. Re-tape morning.
- Best for: Active clients, oily scalps, skin bases, clients who prefer nightly removal.

### Extended Wear (Tape)
- Products: Walker Ultra Hold Tape, Walker Extenda-Bond
- Hold: 1–2 weeks
- Routine: Apply tape full perimeter. Clean and re-tape every 1–2 weeks.
- Best for: Clients wanting longer hold without liquid adhesives.

### Liquid Adhesive (Full Bond)
- Products: Ghost Bond Platinum, Ultra Hold Adhesive
- Hold: 2–4 weeks
- Routine: Apply 3–4 thin layers to scalp. Allow each to go clear.
  Press base into adhesive. Cold-air dry to speed curing.
- Best for: Lace bases, undetectable hairlines, maximum hold.

### Hybrid Bond (Tape + Adhesive)
- Products: Walker Ultra Hold Tape (perimeter) + Ghost Bond XL (front 1cm)
- Hold: 1–2 weeks
- Routine: Tape sides and back perimeter. Thin adhesive layer on front 1cm only.
- Best for: Most clients. Secure hold + undetectable front hairline.

## 9. Before/After Transformation Standards

### Left frame (Before):
- Natural Norwood 3–6 male pattern baldness
- Authentic scalp with visible pores
- Remaining biological side hair clearly visible
- Neutral expression, same wardrobe as after frame
- Clinical neutral lighting

### Right frame (After):
- SAME man — identical face, bone structure, eye color, skin tone
- Seamless hair system restoring natural density
- Hairline with full graduation: 60–65% → 40%, bleached knots, baby hairs
- No visible base edge, lace grid, or dark knots
- SAME lighting, SAME camera angle, SAME wardrobe, SAME expression

### Technical requirements for transformation images:
- Same focal length and aperture on both frames
- Same white balance and color temperature
- Same framing (face occupies same position and size in frame)
- Pixel-level facial identity match (not just "similar" — the same person)
- The only difference should be the hair

## 10. Care & Maintenance

### Daily care:
- Brush gently with a wide-tooth comb or loop brush
- Use sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner
- Air dry when possible. Low heat if blow-drying.
- Avoid sleeping with wet hair (causes tangling and matting)

### Weekly care:
- Deep condition with leave-in treatment
- Clean base with appropriate cleaner (skin vs lace)
- Check attachment bond integrity
- Inspect hairline for lifting

### Monthly care:
- Full system removal, deep clean, and re-attachment
- Trim any grown-out biological hair at perimeter
- Professional styling and blending if possible

## 11. Common Mistakes in Hair System Images

When AI generates images of men with hair systems, these are the most common failures:

1. **Helmet hair**: Too much density (above 120%), hair sits as a solid mass
2. **Plastic doll skin**: Over-airbrushed, no pores, synthetic sheen
3. **Visible base edge**: A seam or ridge where the base ends
4. **Blunt hairline**: No graduation, hair starts at full density instantly
5. **Dark knot dots**: Visible row of dark dots along hairline
6. **Lace grid visible**: Mesh pattern showing through hair
7. **Synthetic shine**: Plastic-like reflection, not natural hair luster
8. **Wrong color at transition**: Hair system color doesn't match biological side hair
9. **Different person**: Before and after frames show different facial features
10. **Unnatural scalp**: No pore texture, uniform color, looks painted on
